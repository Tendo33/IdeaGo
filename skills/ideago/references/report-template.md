# Report template

Sections stay in this order — the decision comes first, evidence last. Translate headings to the
report language. Every claim that rests on evidence gets an inline link or a `[n]` reference into
the evidence list.

```markdown
# <Idea in one line>

> <YYYY-MM-DD> · <app_type> · <target_scenario>

## 1. Recommendation: GO | CAUTION | NO-GO

<2-3 sentences: the verdict and the single strongest reason. Name the entry wedge if there is one.>

**Why now:** <what changed recently, or "nothing obvious has changed — timing is neutral">

**Opportunity score:** 0.xx — pain 0.x · gap 0.x · commercial 0.x · freshness 0.x · competition 0.x

## 2. Pain signals

| theme | intensity | frequency | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| <theme> | high/med/low | high/med/low | [1] [4] |

<One short paragraph on the pattern behind the pain, if there is one.>

## 3. Commercial signals

- **<theme>** — <summary>. Monetization hint: <...>. [2]

Migration: <who is switching from what, and why — or "no switching signals found">

## 4. Whitespace opportunities

### <Opportunity title>
- **Gap:** <why it exists>
- **For:** <target segment>
- **Wedge:** <concrete first product / feature / channel>
- **Evidence:** [3] [5]

**Differentiation angles:** <3-5 bullets, each naming a competitor weakness>

## 5. Competitors

### Direct
| product | what it does | pricing | traction | strengths | weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name](url) | ... | ... | 22k★, updated 2026-09 | ... | ... |

### Adjacent
<same table, or a short list>

<2-3 sentences: how crowded is it, what's the dominant approach.>

## 6. Evidence

1. [<title>](<url>) — <platform>, <date> — "<short snippet>"
2. ...

## 7. Confidence: NN/100

- Sources: <worked> / <tried> (<list failures or degraded sources>)
- <reasons that raised or lowered confidence>
- <uncertainty notes: thin, contradictory, or one-segment evidence>
```
